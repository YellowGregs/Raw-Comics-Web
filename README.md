# Raw Comics Web
Quick list of spots to grab raw manga (Japanese), manhwa (Korean), and manhua (Chinese). Most are free with some paid options or ads, so check it out yourself.

**Note:** If I got anything wrong or misplaced a site, sorry about that, ill will update when I can, and also feel free to open a pull request with fixes or suggestions.

Also, check out the live version of this list (with nicer formatting and sections):  
👉 https://raw-comic.vercel.app/

> **How sites are categorized:** Some official/unofficial sites host mixed comics (manga, manhwa, and manhua together), but translate everything into **one target language** (like Korean manhwa translated into Chinese on a Chinese site), so those sites are sorted under the **language they publish in** rather than Mixed but that could be confusing but i did it anyways.

---

## Official
Legit platforms from publishers and rights holders. Most offer free opening/latest chapters or daily wait-to-read tickets.

### Korean (Manhwa & KR-Translated Comics)
- [comic.naver.com](https://comic.naver.com/) - Naver Webtoon, massive catalog of official Korean originals.
- [page.kakao.com](https://page.kakao.com/) - Kakao Page, huge Korean webtoon and web novel platform.
- [webtoon.kakao.com](https://webtoon.kakao.com/) - Kakao Webtoon, official Korean webtoons with free daily episodes/events.
- [ridibooks.com](https://ridibooks.com/) - RIDI, major Korean ebook/webtoon store (strong romance, fantasy, and BL catalog).
- [lezhin.com/ko](https://www.lezhin.com/ko) - Lezhin Comics (Korean), big catalog of mature, thriller, and BL webtoons.
- [bomtoon.com](https://www.bomtoon.com/) - Bomtoon, official Korean platform focused heavily on romance, BL, and GL.
- [toomics.com](https://www.toomics.com/) - Toomics, official Korean platform with action, drama, and mature titles.

### Chinese (Manhua & CN-Translated Comics)
- [ac.qq.com](https://ac.qq.com/) - Tencent Comics, massive official Chinese library.
- [manhua.bilibili.com](https://manhua.bilibili.com/) - Bilibili Manhua, official platform (also absorbed the former U17 catalog).
- [kuaikanmanhua.com](https://www.kuaikanmanhua.com/) - Kuaikan Manhua, fast updates on trending Chinese titles.
- [iqiyi.com/manhua](https://www.iqiyi.com/manhua/) - iQiyi Manhua, official Chinese webcomics portal.
- [dongmanmanhua.cn](https://www.dongmanmanhua.cn/) - Dongman Manhua, Naver/WEBTOON's official Chinese platform.

### Japanese (Manga & JP-Translated Webtoons)
- [shonenjumpplus.com](https://shonenjumpplus.com/) - Shonen Jump+ (Shueisha), great for Jump series with free first/latest chapters.
- [mangaplus.shueisha.co.jp](https://mangaplus.shueisha.co.jp/) - MANGA Plus (Shueisha), simulpub chapters in multiple languages alongside Japan.
- [tonarinoyj.jp](https://tonarinoyj.jp/) - Tonari no Young Jump (Shueisha), free web magazine (home of *One-Punch Man* raws).
- [zebrack-comic.shueisha.co.jp](https://zebrack-comic.shueisha.co.jp/) - Zebrack (Shueisha), comprehensive Jump/Young Jump portal with daily free tickets.
- [kmanga.kodansha.com](https://kmanga.kodansha.com/) - K MANGA (Kodansha), official English simulpub platform with free starters and points.
- [comic-days.com](https://comic-days.com/) - Comic DAYS (Kodansha), official web platform with magazines, free chapters, and subscriptions.
- [pocket.shonenmagazine.com](https://pocket.shonenmagazine.com/) - Magazine Pocket / Magapoke (Kodansha), Weekly Shonen Magazine & web originals.
- [comic-walker.com](https://comic-walker.com/) - KadoComi / Comic Walker (Kadokawa), loads of free serialized chapters.
- [bookwalker.jp](https://bookwalker.jp/) - BOOK☆WALKER (Kadokawa), digital storefront for raw manga and light novels.
- [sunday-webry.com](https://www.sunday-webry.com/) - Sunday Webry (Shogakukan), official web portal for Shonen Sunday series.
- [ganganonline.com](https://www.ganganonline.com/) - GANGAN ONLINE (Square Enix), free fantasy and anime-adapted manga raws.
- [ganma.jp](https://ganma.jp/web) - GANMA!, ad-supported platform with completely free original web manga.
- [comico.jp](https://www.comico.jp/) - comico, official vertical-scroll manga/webtoon platform in Japanese.
- [cmoa.jp](https://www.cmoa.jp/) - Comic Seymour, one of Japan's largest digital manga stores with frequent free-volume promos.
- [manga.nicovideo.jp](https://manga.nicovideo.jp/) - Nico Nico Seiga / Manga, official Kadokawa/Dwango portal with publisher series and indie raws.
- [comic.pixiv.net](https://comic.pixiv.net/) - pixiv Comic, official web manga hub featuring indie hits and publisher tie-ins.
- [mangaz.com](https://www.mangaz.com/) - Manga Library Z, free full-volume raw manga of out-of-print and classic titles with creator permission.

## Unofficial
Unofficial raw sites and aggregators sorted by the **language hosted on the site**. Domain numbers and TLDs rotate often, and also use a adblocker.

### Japanese (Raw Manga)
- [rawkuma.net](https://rawkuma.net/) - Straight Japanese manga raws, high quality, popular for scanlators (migrated from `.com`).
- [raw.senmanga.com](https://raw.senmanga.com/) - Japanese raw manga, reliable for ongoing magazine chapters.
- [rawlazy.io](https://rawlazy.io/) - Large catalog of Japanese manga raws, updated frequently.
- [weloma.art](https://weloma.art/) - Active Japanese raw reader (successor/clone to older MangaGun/LoveHeaven networks).
- [mangagun.net](https://mangagun.net/) - Fast magazine and digital Japanese raw uploads.
- [klz9.com](https://klz9.com/) - Classic Japanese raw manga aggregator (formerly KL Manga).
- [dl-raw.ac](https://dl-raw.ac/) - Direct download site for full raw Japanese tankobon volumes (ZIP/RAR).

### Korean (Raw Manhwa & KR-Translated Manga)
*(Note: The original Newtoki/Manatoki network permanently shut down; current sites are archive mirrors or rotating-number clones).*
- [toonkor0.org](https://toonkor0.org/) - Long-running Korean webtoon archive/mirror (also rotates through numbered `tkor###.com` domains).
- [wfwf540.com](https://wfwf540.com/) - Wolfdotcom, Korean webtoon aggregator (domain number increments frequently when blocked).
- [joatoon-oh.com](https://joatoon-oh.com/) - Active Newtoki archive mirror for Korean manhwa raws.

### Chinese (Raw Manhua & CN-Translated Manga/Manhwa)
- [manhuagui.com](https://www.manhuagui.com/) - Massive Simplified Chinese archive hosting manhua plus Chinese-translated manga/manhwa.
- [twmanga.com](https://www.twmanga.com/) - Traditional Chinese mirror of Manhuagui for raw manhua and translated manga.
- [colamanga.com](https://www.colamanga.com/) - Chinese comic site with fast updates and a clean vertical reader.
- [baozimh.com](https://baozimh.com/) - Hosts Chinese manhua alongside Japanese manga and Korean manhwa translated into Chinese.
- [dumanwu.com](https://www.dumanwu.com/) - Chinese comic aggregator with manhua and CN-translated series.

### Multi-Language / Raw Torrents
- [nyaa.si](https://nyaa.si/) - Torrent tracker (filter by *Literature - Raw* for untouched Japanese, Korean, and Chinese volume rips).

## Trackers
Places to keep tabs on your reading progress, ratings, and alerts for new releases.

- [anilist.co](https://anilist.co/) - AniList, clean UI and great API/app integrations.
- [myanimelist.net](https://myanimelist.net/) - MyAnimeList, the classic database and list tracker.
- [kitsu.app](https://kitsu.app/) - Kitsu, easy progress tracking and community feed.
- [anime-planet.com](https://www.anime-planet.com/) - Anime-Planet, solid recommendation engine.
- [kenmei.co](https://www.kenmei.co/) - Kenmei, cross-site tracker built specifically for webtoons and manga.
- [mangaupdates.com](https://www.mangaupdates.com/) - MangaUpdates (Baka-Updates), best database for publisher info, raw status, and scanlation groups.
- [comick.dev](https://comick.dev/) - ComicK, great for tracking and discovery.
- [MangaTime app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.mangatime.app) - Mobile app for tracking manga, manhwa, and manhua.
